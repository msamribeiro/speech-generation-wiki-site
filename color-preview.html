F5-TTS: A Fairytaler that Fakes Fluent and Faithful Speech with Flow Matching
May 26, 2026 · 6 min read · TTS flow-matching zero-shot

F5-TTS introduces a fully non-autoregressive TTS system built on flow matching over mel-spectrograms. By concatenating noisy speech with a text representation, the model achieves high-fidelity zero-shot synthesis without an autoregressive language model backbone.

Achieving SIM-o 0.671 on Seed-TTS-eval-en — competitive with closed-source systems — using a simple DiT backbone trained on 95K hours of English and Mandarin speech.

Architecture

The system uses a DiT (Diffusion Transformer) conditioned on character-level text sequences. Unlike prior work relying on phoneme alignment, F5-TTS uses a Voicebox-style infilling objective, treating synthesis as masked speech generation. The flow matching decoder runs 32 Euler steps at inference.

💡 Key finding

Replacing the Gaussian source distribution with a learned prior (OZSpeech) reduces inference to a single step (NFE=1), trained on only 500 hours — no quality loss on WER or SIM.
