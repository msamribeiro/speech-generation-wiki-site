# Speech Generation Wiki — Visual Identity & Theme Guide

## Design Goals

The visual identity should communicate:

- Research synthesis
- Knowledge graphs
- Concept relationships
- Trends and field evolution
- Speech generation research

Avoid generic speech or audio branding (microphones, speakers, audio editors).

The preferred visual metaphor is:

> A knowledge graph connected to speech generation.

# Favicon

## Preferred Direction

Connected Speech Graph

Components:

- Knowledge graph nodes
- Relationship edges
- Speech waveform

Meaning:

- Nodes = concepts
- Edges = relationships
- Waveform = speech generation

Guidelines:

- Recognizable at 16×16
- Simple geometry
- Transparent background when possible
- Navy brand background

# Brand Positioning

The site should feel:

- Research-focused
- Technical
- Structured
- Modern
- Analytical

Avoid:

- Podcast branding
- Consumer audio software aesthetics
- Generic AI startup visuals

Core message:

> A living systematic review and knowledge graph for speech-generation research.
